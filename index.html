    Briefing

    Spread an idea. Beat the paperwork.

    You lead a movement that wants the whole world to agree with it. Nobody has to be conquered. They just have to be convinced.

    1. Pick a starting country. Support grows there and spills over land borders and trade routes by itself.
    2. Spend Clout on upgrades (they work everywhere) and operations (one country, then a cooldown). Click amber bubbles to collect Clout.
    3. The more the world notices you, the faster the World Order drafts its Containment Treaty. Loud tactics fill the Notoriety meter fast.
    4. Win by aligning 70% of the world before the treaty is signed.
    Space pauses. 1, 2, 3 set the speed.
