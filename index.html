    Briefing

    Spread an idea. Beat the paperwork.

    You lead a movement that wants the whole world to agree with it. Nobody has to be conquered. They just have to be convinced.

    1. Pick a starting country. Support grows there and spills over land borders and trade routes by itself.
    1. Spend Clout on upgrades (they work everywhere) and actions (four per domain, one country at a time, then a cooldown). Upgrades come first: the 1st, 2nd and 3rd upgrade in a branch open that domain’s tier-1, tier-2 and tier-3 actions, and the top upgrade unlocks a tier-4 action that can turn the tide. Click amber bubbles to collect Clout. Upgrades come in opposed pairs: commit to Ideology or Economy, and to Military or Diplomacy.
    1. Once you are noticed, the World Order convenes. It recruits member countries, runs campaigns against you, sanctions your trade routes and collects signatures for its Containment Treaty. Every move is announced a few weeks ahead: a Summit in the target country derails it.
    1. Win by holding all 16 countries (60%+ support) at the same time, or by aligning 90% of the world. Hold so many that the World Order can never reach 8 signatures for 26 weeks (while you align 40%+) and it breaks up; after that, countries that stop resisting capitulate, and the last holdouts form a Grand Coalition you must break before it unites the world. When 8 countries sign first, the treaty is ratified and Enforcement drains your support everywhere until you break it or fade away.
    1. Holding is not owning. Countries where you have a foothold but have not settled grow restless, and at 100 unrest they rise up against you. Garrison them with Military or calm them with Pamphlets. You get a short briefing the first time it happens.
    1. Guard your base. Its legitimacy (the crown ring on the map and the meter at the bottom) rises with victories and prosperity and falls with uprisings, lost countries and casualties abroad. At 0 the army takes over and you lose. Once your base reaches 60%, letting it fall below 35% for 10 weeks also loses the game. Actions on your own base restore legitimacy. A Contingency Plan lets you move the base once. Holding many countries costs Clout upkeep.
    Space pauses. 1, 2, 3 set the speed.

    Anonymous gameplay data is collected to improve the game. You can switch it off in Settings.
