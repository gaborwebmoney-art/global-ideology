    Briefing

    Spread an idea. Beat the paperwork.

    You lead a movement that wants the whole world to agree with it. Nobody has to be conquered. They just have to be convinced.

    1. Pick a starting country. Support grows there and spills over land borders and trade routes by itself.
    2. Spend Clout on upgrades (they work everywhere) and operations (one country, then a cooldown). Click amber bubbles to collect Clout. Upgrades come in opposed pairs: commit to Ideology or Economy, and to Military or Diplomacy. Each branch is boosted by a partner (Ideology with Diplomacy, Economy with Military), and its top upgrade is a one-off special action.
    3. Once you are noticed, the World Order convenes. It recruits member countries, runs campaigns against you, sanctions your trade routes and collects signatures for its Containment Treaty. Every move is announced a few weeks ahead: a Summit in the target country derails it.
    4. Win by aligning 70% of the world, or by holding so many countries (60%+ support) that the World Order can never reach 8 signatures for 26 weeks. Lose if 8 countries sign.
    Space pauses. 1, 2, 3 set the speed.
