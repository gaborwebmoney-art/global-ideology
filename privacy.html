Global Ideology
Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-28

Who is responsible

Global Ideology is a strategy game, made and run by an individual developer, Pavel Gurishev (Budapest, Hungary), who is the data controller for the data described here. You can reach the developer at the contact email shown on the game's Google Play page, or with More → Report a bug in the game.

This policy covers the Android app, the web game at https://gaborwebmoney-art.github.io/global-ideology/ and its beta at /beta/.

The short version

  • You can play without an account. Your game then stays on your device.
  • An account needs an email address and a password, and lets you continue a game on any device. Accounts are for players aged 16 or older.
  • The game sends anonymous gameplay data (you can switch this off), crash reports, and bug reports only if you send them.
  • The public ranking is opt-in. Only the ranking name you choose and your scores are shown.
  • There are no ads, no advertising or analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps or sites, and your data is never sold.
  • You can delete your account and its data inside the game (Menu (⋯) → Settings → Account and data → Delete account) or on the account deletion page.

What stays on your device

The game keeps its save, settings (language, text size, map view, news pause, data-sharing choice) and a random anonymous device ID in the app's local storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes all of it. If you play without an account, the running game is also kept there so it can continue after the app is closed.

Accounts

If you create an account we store your email address and a hashed password (we never see or store the password itself), and your saved game. The email address is used only to sign you in, to send password-reset emails, and to contact you about your account. Accounts and saves are handled by Supabase (see below).

You must be at least 16 years old to create an account. Younger players can play without one.

Global ranking (optional)

You can join the public ranking after logging in. If you do, the ranking shows the ranking name you choose (please do not use your real name), an optional country you choose, and your winning scores (difficulty, score, stars, weeks, start country). Anyone can see the ranking. Leaving the ranking (Menu, your ranking name, then Leave the ranking) deletes your ranking name and scores.

Who processes the data

  • Supabase (database and sign-in): accounts, saves, ranking, gameplay data, crash and bug reports. The project is hosted in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • GitHub Pages (hosting of the web game and these pages): GitHub may log technical connection data such as IP addresses when the web game or app loads.
  • Google Play distributes the Android app and handles installation data under Google's own policies.

We do not share data with anyone else, and we do not sell it.

How long we keep it

  • Account, saved game, ranking entry: until you delete your account (or leave the ranking).
  • Gameplay data, crash reports and bug reports: up to 24 months. When you delete your account, any of these still attached to your account ID are detached from it, so they are anonymous afterwards.
  • Data on your device: until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Most of this you can do yourself in the game; for anything else contact the developer. If you think your rights are not respected you can complain to your data protection authority; in Hungary this is the Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH, naih.hu).

Legal bases: the account and save are needed to provide the service you asked for (contract); crash reports and gameplay data serve our legitimate interest in a working, balanced game; the ranking is based on your consent, which you can withdraw by leaving it.

Children

The game is not designed for children. Accounts and the ranking are for players aged 16 or older. Without an account no name, email or other identifying data is collected; anonymous gameplay data and crash reports described above still apply unless gameplay data is switched off.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version replaces this page and the date at the top changes. Material changes will also be announced in the game.
